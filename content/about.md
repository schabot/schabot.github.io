I’m Steven Chabot, and this site is my base on the web.

I’m using it for a few specific things:

- publishing finished thoughts instead of leaving everything in drafts
- documenting projects in a way that is useful later
- keeping a clean, durable record of what I am building and learning

The site itself is intentionally simple: Hugo for the generator, Gokarna for the theme, and plain markdown for most of the content.
