# Steven Chabot

This site is where I publish work that is worth keeping around: projects, writing, and notes as they become concrete enough to share.

## What you'll find here

- Short posts about things I am building or figuring out
- Project write-ups with decisions, tradeoffs, and results
- A lightweight home for updates instead of scattering them across platforms

## Current direction

Right now this site is being rebuilt with Hugo and the Gokarna theme. The goal is a fast, simple personal site that is easy to maintain and pleasant to read.

- Browse [posts](/posts/) once publishing starts
